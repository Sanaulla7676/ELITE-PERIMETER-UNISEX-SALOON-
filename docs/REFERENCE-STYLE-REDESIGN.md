# Elite Perimeter Reference-Led Website Design

## Source references reviewed
Six supplied images were reviewed. The uploaded K Edit three-treatment board appears twice; the five unique visual directions are:
1. Neutral hair-extension editorial: oversized split typography, warm sand background, long-hair image, compact supporting copy.
2. Dark hair-treatment offer: dark salon backdrop, centered product/service heading, benefit labels and a prominent price block.
3. Organic head-spa collage: rounded/curved image windows, warm taupe palette, large serif title, simple treatment labels.
4. Three-treatment board: three stacked horizontal treatment panels, dark cocoa cards, alternating image/text sides and organic curved separators.
5. Burgundy summer treatment board: plum/maroon cards, high-contrast white text, multiple service bands and restrained warm accents.

## Typography
- Display serif: Georgia, with Times New Roman fallback.
- Supporting/UI sans: Poppins, with Arial fallback.
- No script or decorative display font is used. Competitor names and logos are not reused.

## Application across the site
- Homepage: editorial hero plus three horizontal treatment bands inspired by the stacked treatment reference.
- Service directory: oversized headline and category-led navigation.
- Hair service pages: warm sand editorial treatment with a large split display word, hair image and supporting labels.
- Facial service pages: soft spa palette, curved image window, consultation/treatment/after-care callouts and a genuine-client portfolio slot.
- Waxing service pages: asymmetric service image and clear area/preparation/after-care blocks.
- Threading service pages: restrained editorial title with detail-focused split panel.
- Nail service pages: warm mauve/taupe collage treatment with care/finish/product blocks.
- Category pages: category-specific compositions and backgrounds.
- Package index: dark burgundy header and alternating treatment-card panels.
- Package details: dark offer-style hero with the printed combo price shown only for the complete package.
- Blog index and articles: editorial magazine hierarchy with oversized headlines, numbered content and plum takeaway panels.
- About, contact and location pages: same typography, spacing, buttons and warm neutral palette.

## Content and compliance notes
- Existing route slugs, editorial copy, menu inclusions, SEO metadata, structured data and printed package prices are preserved.
- No unverified standalone price is inferred from a combo rate.
- Botox Spa and Hyder Facial remain subject to owner verification.
- Before/after sections are clearly marked as pending genuine client photographs. No AI-generated transformation is represented as customer proof.
- Existing category images remain illustrative stock/category imagery, not photographs of Elite Perimeter's customers or completed work.
- The reference brands, competitor logos, phone numbers, locations and promotional offers are not copied into the website.
