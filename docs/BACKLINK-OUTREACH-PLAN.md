# Elite Perimeter — Local Backlink & Digital PR Plan

## Purpose

This plan implements the backlink principles in the supplied SEO PDF: start with legitimate business profiles and relevant directories, then earn contextually relevant links from local publishers and journalists. It is not a bulk directory submission list. For profile-ready NAP data, platform-by-platform claim steps, owner approval gates and the evidence tracker, see [FOUNDATIONAL-CITATION-KIT.md](./FOUNDATIONAL-CITATION-KIT.md).

**Important distinction:** adding a link from the salon website to another website is an outbound link. A backlink is created only when the external publisher or platform places a link that points back to Elite Perimeter. No external publisher has been represented as having linked to the salon by this project.

## What makes a valuable link

Prioritise opportunities by:
1. Local relevance to Bengaluru, Ullal, Jnana Ganga Nagar and the salon/beauty category.
2. Editorial independence and a real audience, rather than a website created only to sell SEO links.
3. A genuine business profile or a useful editorial reference that users can click.
4. Accurate business details and a destination page that answers the searcher's question.
5. A link that is naturally earned. Never buy a followed link or demand a keyword-rich anchor.

Do not rank prospects solely by third-party Domain Authority/Domain Rating numbers. Those are vendor metrics, not Google's ranking score, and a high metric on an irrelevant or low-quality website does not make it a good prospect.

## Outreach angles to test

1. **A transparent salon package comparison:** explain that the salon's printed prices refer to complete combinations and that waxing/threading body areas vary between packages. This is directly supported by the printed menu data.
2. **Questions to ask before a first threading appointment:** a consumer checklist on shape, areas, sensitivity and preparation.
3. **How to choose a facial without relying on a service name alone:** ask about actual products, steps, sensitivities and inclusions.
4. **A customer hygiene question checklist:** neutral advice about asking salons how work areas and reusable tools are prepared. Do not claim a particular certification or standard unless the salon can prove it.
5. **A bridal package inclusion guide:** accurately identify what the ₹4,999 poster includes and what it does not list, such as bridal makeup or makeup trials.

## Rules to keep the link profile safe

- Do not buy links sold as "DA 80", "100 backlinks", "guest-post bundles" or guaranteed ranking packages.
- Do not submit the salon to hundreds of irrelevant, duplicate or low-quality directories.
- Do not automate comment/forum links or create reciprocal-link pages.
- Do not make a free service, product or payment conditional on a followed link.
- For genuine paid sponsorship/advertising, require the publisher to qualify links appropriately (for example, rel="sponsored") and label the content clearly.
- Do not use exact-match anchors repeatedly. Natural brand and descriptive citations are preferable.
- Do not manufacture reviews, press mentions, awards, ratings or journalist quotes.
- Check every listing for the correct name, address, phone and canonical website URL; keep the NAP consistent.

Google's official spam policy explicitly includes buying/selling links for ranking, automated link creation, excessive exchanges, low-quality directory links and paid editorial links that pass ranking credit: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
