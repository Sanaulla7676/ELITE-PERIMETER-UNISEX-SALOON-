## Page design principles

- Editorial premium visual style: warm ivory, deep plum and restrained champagne accents.
- Responsive header and clear mobile navigation.
- One clear H1 and one main call-to-action per page.
- Service details and decision information appear before long promotional copy.
- Price information is explicit: package prices only where printed; standalone rates are not invented.
- Local address, map link and contact method are easy to find.
- Service pages link to their category hub and related services.
- Photo areas currently show visible placeholders; replace them with real salon photography, not a fabricated image of the shop or staff.
