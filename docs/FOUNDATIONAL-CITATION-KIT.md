## 3. Four priority foundational profiles

### A. Google Business Profile

**Official entry:** https://business.google.com/in/business-profile/  
**Add/claim workflow:** https://business.google.com/add

Steps:

1. Sign in with the Google account the owner intends to keep as the long-term business owner.
2. Search Google Maps for the exact business name and Bengaluru before adding anything.
3. If the correct profile exists and is unverified, use the claim flow. If it is verified by someone else, request ownership rather than creating a duplicate.
4. Verify the business using the method Google offers for this specific listing. Do not assume a verification method will be available.
5. Check the business name, map pin, category, address, phone, actual opening hours and service details.
6. Add the permanent HTTPS website URL only after deployment.
7. Upload real exterior signage, interior and service photographs. Use original photographs; do not present stock imagery as the salon.
8. Save the public Google Maps/profile URL and a screenshot of the website field in the tracker below.

Google states that eligible businesses can add or claim a profile without charge, and customers can find verified profiles on Search and Maps. Existing unverified listings can be claimed; an already verified profile may require an ownership request. See: https://support.google.com/business/answer/2911778

## 5. External link and digital PR campaign

A profile listing is a foundational citation and customer-discovery asset. An editorial backlink is earned only when an independent external publisher publishes a page that links to the salon's live website. A link from the salon site to a newspaper, supplier or social profile is an outbound link, not an inbound backlink.

### Editorial story angles grounded in the actual business menu

1. **How to compare salon combo packages before booking:** compare the printed package price, every included service and the waxing/threading coverage. This is the strongest local consumer angle because the menu has several similar combinations with different inclusions.
2. **Questions to ask before a first eyebrow-threading visit:** shape preference, areas included, sensitivity and pre-appointment questions.
3. **How to choose a facial by checking products and steps:** explain why a facial name alone does not prove a specific formula or guaranteed result.
4. **A customer checklist for salon hygiene questions:** discuss reusable tools, work surfaces and single-use items as questions for customers to ask. Do not claim a certification or infection-prevention result.
5. **Planning pre-wedding salon appointments from the printed package list:** accurately state what the ₹4,999 menu item lists and that the poster does not include bridal makeup or trials. Resolve the Bridal Facial / Hyder Facial wording before giving an expert quote.

### Publisher qualification checklist

Before pitching or accepting a link, check:

- The publisher is active and has a real readership relevant to Bengaluru, beauty, lifestyle or local consumers.
- The proposed article is useful to readers and independently edited.
- The story facts can be substantiated by the current menu and owner/stylist knowledge.
- The requested link destination genuinely supports the article.
- The publisher is not selling a package of keyword-rich links or promising rankings.
- Paid sponsorship is labelled, and paid links are appropriately qualified (for example, `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow`).

Do not buy bulk links, automated forum/comment links, reciprocal-link schemes or low-quality directory placements. Google's spam policies specifically identify paid ranking links, automated link creation and manipulative link schemes as violations: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
