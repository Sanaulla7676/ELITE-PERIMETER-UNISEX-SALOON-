# Information to collect before full indexing

This build is intentionally based only on the salon posters and current GBP details supplied in the conversation. The following facts would make service pages stronger and more useful. Do not invent them.

## For each service

- Exact service name as staff use it.
- Individual standalone price (or a confirmed note that pricing is quoted in person).
- Duration / appointment time, if reliable.
- Exact inclusions and body areas.
- Products or brands used, if the salon wants to publish them and can verify them.
- Step-by-step process, only as accurately confirmed by staff.
- Who performs the service and their genuine qualifications/experience, if publishable.
- Preparation and aftercare instructions supplied by the salon/product manufacturer.
- 2–4 original photos of the real service setup or completed work, with permission.
- Common questions customers actually ask.

## Two labels to verify

1. **Botox Spa:** Is this Hair Botox, a conditioning treatment or another salon service? Do not write about injectable Botox.
2. **Hyder Facial:** Is the poster spelling correct, or is it intended to say Hydra Facial? Confirm the actual product/process and name.

## Business facts to reconfirm

- Business name exactly as on storefront/signage.
- Full address and postal PIN.
- Phone number and whether it accepts calls/WhatsApp/SMS.
- Opening hours for every day, including holidays.
- Whether the salon actually provides at-home services in any of the listed service areas.
- Whether old public Google Maps listings under a different name belong to the same salon/profile.

## Photo checklist

- Exterior signboard and entrance.
- Reception and waiting area.
- Haircut / hair spa work.
- Facial treatment room and product setup.
- Waxing service setup.
- Threading station.
- Manicure and pedicure setup.
- Staff at work (with consent).
- Finished client results only with written permission.
